about

I’m primarily a technical builder and serial founder. Outhad AI is my third company. My first, FractalXR, was acquired by Boxally when I was 18, straight out of school. outLfy came second. My academic background is in applied math, CS, and quantitative economics, with splashes of philosophy, physics, and psychology.

Lately I’ve been designing Franz, a functional programming language built in C with LLVM native compilation — a tiny, keyword-free functional core with prototype-oriented objects, Rust-like safety, lexical scoping, and deterministic replay.

I spend a lot of time building Convertive by Outhad AI, reading works by Jeffrey Archer, Jaun Elia, and J.K. Rowling, and playing and watching cricket.
